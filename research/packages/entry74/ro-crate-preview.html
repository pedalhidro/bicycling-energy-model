A afirmação

The incumbent holds, decisively: the deployed grade-local eps_geo wins every treated-map cell on both DTMs and both regions (3.31 med|d%| at the Sao Paulo sigma-30 n = 16 cell vs 5.11 for the published flat eps-2 and 10.24 for the regionally honest eps_SP = 0.2385 — the lower regional constant loses ~5 pp because the treated-chain bias is already positive). Entry 45's grade-inverse deficit trails by ~3 pp (its k/s term under-credits the shallow band treated chains live in) and the occupancy mechanism curve does not transfer from route cells to edges. On RAW chains the ordering inverts and flat eps-2 wins: noise manufactures steep micro-descents that grade-local policies refuse to credit — adaptive recovery and untreated noise do not mix. eps = 0 prices the descent term as worst everywhere. Five policies, identical edge skeleton, all constants frozen from producing CSVs; no candidate fitted on this experiment's energies.
