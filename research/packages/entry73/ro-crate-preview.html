A afirmação

The direction-count penalty is chain-shaped: on the smoothed 5 m survey it is essentially all path-length inflation (len_ratio 1.2737 at n = 4, 1.0543 at n = 8), on raw FABDEM essentially all noise (the lattice path reads 1.417x the polyline's h+ at n = 8). eF4 — the per-edge noise deduction at each chain's MEASURED c(tau=2) pin — beats eF2 on all six DEM chains and is the best deployable cost on FABDEM (41.62 vs 48.23 med|d%|). The deadband keeps a unique share at edge grain: F3 on the raw chain beats eF2 on the sigma-30-treated map on both populations (5.67 vs 9.21; 7.01 vs 11.49) — the filter can move into the map, but not all of it. The flat-vs-geometric epsilon contest is chain-dependent: grade-local wins on smoothed chains, flat on raw FABDEM. The portal deck helps 860/883 span-touched rides at sigma 10. Two populations all-arms-complete (IGC 1,034; FABDEM 1,844); every fitted constant read from its producing CSV; c pins measured from geometry only, worst disagreement with Entry 71's rates 0.14 m/km.
